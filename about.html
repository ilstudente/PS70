About

This is my documentation site for PS70: Introduction to Digital Fabrication at Harvard University.

Throughout this course, I explore the intersection of design, engineering, and fabrication. From 2D design and CNC machining to programming microcontrollers and building networked systems, each project documents my journey in learning to create physical objects through digital tools.

The site showcases weekly assignments covering topics like inputs and outputs, machine building, and culminates in a final project that synthesizes these skills.
