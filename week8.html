Vacuum Forming

We then vacuum-formed the CNC-cut shape. The first try didn't work, because the heat was too low and the plastic never softened enough to take the shape of the mold. A second, hotter pull worked.

First attempt: too-low heat, the plastic didn't soften enough to conform to the mold.

Second attempt: plastic fully drawn over the CNC-cut Austria outline.

The finished vacuum form together with the CNC-cut piece, in front of some actual Austrian mountains.
