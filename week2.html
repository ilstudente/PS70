Step-by-Step Documentation

Note: The model is at a scale of 1:10

1Create the Bottle Body Base

Sketch a circle with radius R=4.5mm, then extrude it 28mm to form the main body of the bottle.

2Shell and Add Upper Body

Shell out the body with 0.5mm thickness. Add the narrower upper part of the body with R=3.5mm and shell thickness of 0.5mm.

3Connect Body Sections

Draw a smooth surface connecting the two body sections and rotate it around the Z-axis. Smoothen out the upper part of the bottleneck and extend it slightly by 1mm.

4Add Bottom Structure

Add structure to the bottom of the bottle using concentric circles (R=3.5mm and R=1.5mm). Extrude and connect them. Finally, smoothen all remaining edges with R=1mm.

5Create the Bottle Cap Base

Start with a circle (R=3.5mm) and a smaller concentric circle (R=3mm). Extrude the annulus 2mm to form the cap base.

6Add Narrow Cap Section

Add the narrow part of the bottle cap by extruding an annulus of concentric circles (R=1.5mm and R=1mm).

7Connect Cap Sections

Connect the wide and narrow extruded annuli with a new surface and round the edges with R=1mm.

8Add Band Attachment Hooks

Construct the tangent plane on one side of the narrow part of the cap. Draw a sketch of circles and extrude inward (R=1mm, R=1.5mm, extrusion=0.5mm). Round edges with R=0.5mm, then mirror this to the other side of the cap.

9Create the Band

Create a sketch of a curve connecting the two band handles. Make an inset version of this curve (0.15mm) and extrude symmetrically in both directions (0.5mm).

10Define Components and Joint

Define each of the two parts (bottle body and cap) as separate components in Fusion 360. Create a joint that allows translating the cap up and down relative to the bottle (range=2mm).
